About SetuSight

One Platform for the Complete Bridge Lifecycle

Conventional bridge inspection often relies on manual site visits, paper records, and information scattered across departments and contractors. Findings are hard to compare over time, and maintenance decisions are made without a complete picture of a structure's history.

SetuSight brings together the full lifecycle in one system:

  • Bridge inventory
  • Structural information
  • Inspection records
  • AI-assisted crack detection
  • Health assessment
  • Maintenance history
  • Contractor management
Reactive Maintenance → Data-Driven Preventive Maintenance

Core Features

Everything You Need to Manage Bridge Health

01

AI Crack Detection

Computer vision analyzes bridge inspection images to identify and localize visible cracks.

  • Image-based inspection
  • Crack detection
  • Crack severity analysis
  • Inspection records
02

Bridge Asset Management

A centralized digital record for every bridge in the network.

  • Bridge ID & name
  • Location & bridge type
  • Construction year & design life
  • Material, authority & contractor
  • Environment & traffic load
03

Health Assessment

Inspection results are combined with bridge-specific parameters to generate an AI-assisted health and risk assessment, supporting maintenance prioritization — not a prediction of exact structural failure.

  • Crack condition
  • Bridge age & material
  • Environment & traffic load
  • Inspection & maintenance history
04

Maintenance Management

The complete maintenance workflow, from requirement to closure.

  • Maintenance requirements
  • Work assignment
  • Contractor allocation
  • Repair status & evidence

Access & Roles

One Platform. Three Roles.

Admin

System-wide control.

  • Manage users
  • Manage bridges
  • View inspections
  • Manage maintenance
  • View analytics
  • Generate reports

Inspector

Field inspection and assessment.

  • View assigned bridges
  • Conduct inspections
  • Upload images
  • Run AI crack detection
  • Record inspection results
  • Submit reports

Contractor

Maintenance execution.

  • View assigned work
  • View maintenance requirements
  • Update repair status
  • Upload repair evidence
  • Track completed work

User accounts are created and managed by the Admin. There is no public registration.
